## Technical specification to send with every quote

Specify **genuine NXP NTAG213 or NTAG216**, **13.56 MHz**, **NFC Forum Type 2**, **ISO/IEC 14443-A**, and **NDEF URL encoding** in a passive battery-free ring. NTAG213 provides **144 bytes** of user memory; NTAG216 provides **888 bytes**. The NFC reader's field supplies power. These details are established by [NXP's product documentation](https://www.nxp.com/products/NTAG213_215_216) and [datasheet](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/NTAG213_215_216.pdf).

For a first AxonRing product, a short HTTPS URL to a customer-editable profile/contact page is the simplest interoperable use. Contact fields can live on the profile page; a larger on-tag vCard depends on tag capacity and phone handling. NTAG215 is also a Type 2 option (504 bytes), though it is not necessary for a short URL.

A passive NTAG alone does not create bank-card payment capability, secure user authentication, universal door/car access, GPS tracking, health measurements, or phone unlocking. Access readers require the correct chip family and authorized enrollment; Tesla and payment systems require their own compatible credentials. A URL that opens a payment page is a web link, not an EMV payment ring. Any phone automation depends on the phone, app, or configured shortcut. Validate advertised functions on the supported phone models.

## Sample acceptance and landed-cost worksheet

1. Verify tag model / user memory / NDEF and NXP originality signature with a suitable NFC reader/app. Test URL read and rewrite on the actual NFC-enabled iPhone and Android models planned for support. Keep rings writable during QA; agree any final lock/activation policy separately.
2. Inspect three sizes with calipers against the supplier's diameter chart; assess comfort, ring width, finish, ceramic/epoxy defects, and reader performance while worn. Request evidence for any published water-resistance rating.
3. Place a test fulfillment order only after SKU, destination, and shipping terms are agreed; evaluate actual packaging, tracking, dispatch time, delivery, and wrong-size/defect handling before accepting customer payments.
4. Obtain written per-size inventory and substitutions policy. Launch a limited color/design range with the tested sizes; use a fit guide and realistic exchanges policy.
5. Compare **per-order landed product cost** = unit ring + engraving/encoding + packaging + domestic transfer/agent handling + allocated storage + pick/pack + tracked customer shipping + nonrecoverable import duty/tax + expected defect/replacement cost. Confirm whether quoted delivery is DDP or DAP and whether taxes are included.
6. Compare contribution after payment fees and expected refunds/exchanges. **Affordable acquisition cost** = selling revenue net of sales tax − landed product cost − payment fees − refund/exchange reserve − target contribution. Do not substitute listing price or a bulk unit tier for a destination-specific one-order landed quote.
